# Code of conduct

We want Prime Env Harness to be a place where people can ask questions, share
environments, and disagree about design without being treated badly.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique code, designs, and ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with people working in a second language.
- Keep discussion on topic and useful: reproducible bug reports, concrete proposals,
  and constructive review.
- Respect privacy. Do not share other people's personal information, credentials, or
  private run data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized language or
imagery, doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and pull requests, and may
temporarily or permanently ban people who break these rules. To report a problem,
use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and mark it as a
conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly through their GitHub profile.

This policy applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, and discussions.
